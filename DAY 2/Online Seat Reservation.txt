Online Seat Reservation

A system has limited seats available.
For each booking request, check seat availability.
If seats are available, confirm booking and reduce seat count.
If seats are not available, reject the booking.

    booking={
        [customer:01, seat:2],
        [customer:02, seat:3],
        [customer:03, seat:1],
        [customer:04, seat:4]

    END FUNCTION
