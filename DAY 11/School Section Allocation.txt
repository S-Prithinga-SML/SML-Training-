School Section Allocation

For each student:
If marks ≥ 90 → Section A
If 75–89 → Section B
Else → Section C
Each section has limited capacity.
If section full → assign next available section.

Track:
Students per section
Overflow handling

Requires:
Capacity tracking
Conditional fallback

    marks=90
